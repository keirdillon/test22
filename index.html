Common questions

A little more clarity, before we meet.

Who is Junonia Financial designed for?

Our core focus is people approaching or entering retirement, especially those navigating substantial financial decisions in their 60s. We can also discuss whether another member of our professional network is a better fit for a different need.

Do you only work with people in their 60s?

No. The seventh decade is a strategic focus, not a strict age limit. Needs vary by household, and the first conversation is an opportunity to determine fit.

Does your approach require buying an annuity?

No single product is appropriate for everyone. We begin with your goals and financial circumstances, discuss options and tradeoffs, and evaluate what may be suitable. Insurance and annuity products have specific costs, limitations and risks.

Is Junonia Financial independent of Coastal Wealth and MassMutual?

Junonia Financial is a separate brand associated with financial professionals at Coastal Wealth / MassMutual. Specific legal affiliations, registrations, and service disclosures should be verified in the final, compliance-approved materials.

Our story

Born from an experience no one should have to navigate alone.

For Tom Bell, the importance of thinking differently about retirement income is personal.

Why Tom built Junonia

One experience changed the way he thought about income.

After 22 years at AT&T, Tom took an early retirement and relied in part on the company's dividend income. When the dividend was dramatically reduced, he experienced firsthand the vulnerability of depending too heavily on one income source.

“I don't want them ever to experience what I did.”

That experience helped shape his focus on diversified retirement-income planning, education, and the people behind each decision.

Why the name matters

The rare shell. A meaningful discovery.

The Junonia shell is an uncommon find on Sanibel Island. It inspired a brand built around a similar feeling: seeing an unfamiliar possibility more clearly, and understanding what matters to you.

For Junonia, it's a reminder that thoughtful planning begins with listening, looking carefully, and discovering options you may not have considered.
