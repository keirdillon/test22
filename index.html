The story behind Junonia

A rare name.
A purposeful perspective.

A personal experience changed the way Tom Bell looks at retirement. Junonia was built to help other families approach that transition with greater understanding.

Explore our philosophy ↗
A firsthand lesson

What happens to your income changes everything.

After a 22-year career at AT&T, Tom took early retirement. When a major dividend was reduced, he experienced how quickly an anticipated retirement income stream could change.

That experience reshaped his thinking. Retirement isn't only about the size of a portfolio. It's also about how income is structured, where risk lives and what happens when reality differs from the plan.

Today, his work is centered on helping clients ask those questions before they need the answers.
